Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Document Studio ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how our app handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

Document Studio is designed with privacy first. We collect minimal data:

  • Files you upload: Documents, images, and PDFs you select for conversion are processed locally on your device or sent to the Claude AI API for AI-powered features (OCR, summarize, translate, redact). Files are not stored on our servers.
  • Camera: If you use the Camera Scan feature, the camera feed is processed entirely on your device. No camera data is transmitted.
  • Usage data: We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Files sent to AI features are processed by Anthropic's Claude API and are subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • We do not sell, share, or rent your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

All document conversions happen on your device or via the Anthropic API. We do not store your files, documents, or any personal information on our own servers.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: Used only for the Camera Scan feature to photograph documents.
  • Storage: Used to read files you select and save converted documents to your device.
  • Internet: Required for AI-powered features (OCR, summarize, translate, redact) which use the Claude API.

5. Children's Privacy

Document Studio is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact Us
